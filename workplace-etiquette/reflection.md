# Workplace Etiquette Reflection

## STAR Technique Analysis

### Situation
During my 12-month internship at Coder/Solutions, I was immersed in a professional IT workplace environment where demonstrating appropriate workplace etiquette was essential for successful integration and performance. This included adherence to attendance standards, professional conduct, communication protocols, and workplace ethics.

### Task
My responsibility was to understand and apply professional workplace etiquette standards consistently throughout my internship. This involved maintaining perfect attendance and punctuality, demonstrating professional behavior in all interactions, adhering to workplace policies, and building positive professional relationships with colleagues and supervisors.

### Action
- **Attendance Management:** Maintained 100% attendance record with consistent punctuality (08:00-16:30 daily)
- **Professional Conduct:** Demonstrated respectful communication, collaborative attitude, and appropriate workplace behavior
- **Documentation Compliance:** Completed all required reports and attendance registers accurately and on time
- **Meeting Etiquette:** Participated actively in daily stand-ups and team meetings with prepared contributions
- **Workplace Integration:** Adapted to company culture and norms while maintaining professional standards
- **Feedback Reception:** Actively sought and implemented mentor feedback for continuous improvement

### Result
- Achieved perfect attendance record throughout internship period
- Received consistent positive feedback from mentor on reliability and professionalism
- Successfully integrated into workplace team and culture
- Built reputation as dependable and professional intern
- Developed strong foundation of workplace etiquette for future career
- Enhanced professional reputation through consistent high-standard conduct

## Key Workplace Etiquette Learnings

### Professional Communication

### Workplace Integration

- Professional relationships require balance of friendliness and boundaries
- Observing and learning from experienced colleagues accelerates adaptation
