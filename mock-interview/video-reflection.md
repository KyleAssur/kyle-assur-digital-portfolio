# Mock Interview Video Reflection

## STAR Technique Analysis

### Situation
I participated in a recorded mock interview session designed to simulate real-world job interview conditions for ICT positions. The interview covered both technical and behavioral questions relevant to entry-level developer and systems analyst roles.

### Task
My objective was to demonstrate professional interview skills, including clear communication of my qualifications, effective response to technical questions, appropriate professional demeanor, and the ability to articulate my value proposition to potential employers in the technology sector.

### Action
- **Professional Preparation:** Dressed in business attire and prepared mentally for the interview scenario
- **Technical Readiness:** Reviewed key programming concepts and project experiences
- **Behavioral Examples:** Prepared specific instances demonstrating relevant soft skills
- **Active Listening:** Carefully listened to each question before formulating responses
- **STAR Application:** Structured behavioral responses using Situation-Task-Action-Result framework
- **Engagement:** Maintained eye contact, confident posture, and professional tone throughout

### Result
- Successfully completed the mock interview covering 10+ common interview questions
- Received positive feedback on professional presentation and communication clarity
- Demonstrated strong understanding of technical concepts from my coursework
- Effectively articulated my project experience with StudyBuddy Hub development
- Identified specific areas for further development and refinement
