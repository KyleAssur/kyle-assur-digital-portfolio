# Professional Networking Reflection

## STAR Technique Analysis

### Situation
During my Work Integrated Learning internship at Coder/Solutions, I had the opportunity to develop professional networking skills within a real IT workplace environment. This included building relationships with mentors, colleagues, and industry professionals while working on the StudyBuddy Hub project.

### Task
My objective was to establish professional connections, learn from experienced IT professionals, understand workplace dynamics, and build a foundation for future career networking. This involved effective communication, professional conduct, and active participation in the workplace community.

### Action
- **Mentor Engagement:** Regular meetings and feedback sessions with assigned mentor
- **Team Integration:** Active participation in daily stand-ups and team activities
- **Professional Conduct:** Consistent demonstration of workplace etiquette and reliability
- **Skill Demonstration:** Showcasing technical abilities through project deliverables
- **Relationship Building:** Developing professional rapport with colleagues and supervisors
- **Industry Learning:** Absorbing knowledge about IT industry practices and standards

### Result
- Established strong professional relationship with mentor receiving positive performance feedback
- Successfully integrated into workplace team dynamics and collaboration processes
- Developed reputation for reliability and quality work delivery
- Gained valuable industry insights and career guidance
- Built foundation for future professional references and recommendations
- Enhanced understanding of IT workplace culture and expectations

## Key Networking Learnings

### Professional Relationship Building
- Importance of consistent reliability and follow-through
- Value of active listening and learning from experienced professionals
- Benefits of showing genuine interest in others' work and expertise

### Workplace Integration
- Significance of understanding and adapting to company culture

### Career Development
