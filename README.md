## 🚀 Featured Project: StudyBuddy Hub

**StudyBuddy Hub** is an online platform designed for university students to share study materials, form virtual study groups, and get academic support from peers.

### Key Features:
- Resource sharing for notes, past papers, and assignments
- Module-based study groups with chat functionality
- Q&A forum for academic support
- Mobile-friendly design for South African students
