# Interview Preparation Materials

## Common Interview Questions Prepared

### 1. "Tell me about yourself"
**Response Framework:**
- Brief personal introduction
- Educational background and current studies
- Relevant skills and experience
- Career interests and goals

### 2. "Why do you want to work for our company?"
**Preparation Strategy:**
- Research company values and projects
- Align personal skills with company needs
- Mention specific products or services of interest
- Connect to long-term career goals

### 3. "What are your strengths?"
**Key Strengths Identified:**
- **Adaptability:** Quickly learning new technologies and processes
- **Team Collaboration:** Experience in group projects and retail teamwork
- **Problem-Solving:** Analytical approach from programming background
- **Communication:** Clear explanation of technical concepts

### 4. "What are your weaknesses?"
**Professional Development Areas:**
- Public speaking to large audiences
- Advanced project management methodologies
- Specific technical frameworks (with learning plan)

### 5. "Where do you see yourself in 5 years?"
**Career Path Vision:**
- Junior developer to mid-level specialist
- Technical skills development in specific areas
- Potential team leadership aspirations
- Continuous learning and certification goals

## Behavioral Questions Strategy

### STAR Method Application:
- **Situation:** Context of the experience
- **Task:** Specific responsibility or challenge
- **Action:** Steps taken and skills applied
- **Result:** Outcome and learning achieved

### Example Scenarios Prepared:
- Group project challenges and resolutions
- Customer service experiences from retail work
- Technical problem-solving in academic projects
- Time management during internship
