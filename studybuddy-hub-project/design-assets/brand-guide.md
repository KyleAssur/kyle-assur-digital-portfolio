## Brand Story

### What Are We About?
StudyBuddy Hub is an online platform designed for university and college students to easily share and access study materials, form virtual study groups, and get academic support from peers. The platform operates in the education technology (ed-tech) industry and focuses on creating a supportive online environment for collaborative learning.

What makes us special is that we're created by students, for students, focusing on local institutions to ensure relevant and useful content.

### Vision Statement
> "To become the leading online community for students across South Africa to learn, collaborate, and succeed together — one note and one question at a time."

### Mission Statement
> "Our mission is to empower students by providing a simple and effective platform where they can share resources, connect with peers, and support each other's academic journey."

### Origin

#### The Spark
The idea came from personal experience as a student, noticing the difficulty in finding past papers, good notes, or connecting with classmates when struggling. Many students work alone, even when collaboration could make learning easier.

#### Idea Evolution
The concept grew from a basic file-sharing system into a full platform after recognizing the need for organized study groups and a judgment-free space for academic questions.

## Target Market

### Customer Profile
Our primary customer is the first-year tertiary student (18–22 years old) in South Africa, facing academic, financial, and social challenges. They rely heavily on mobile devices for learning and need affordable, localized resources.

Our secondary audience consists of senior students who share resources or mentor peers.

### Persona Profiles

#### Primary - Sipho Maddala
Introverted, anxious, and resourceful. Needs reliable notes and study groups. Struggles to connect with peers and cannot afford paid platforms.

#### Primary - Mary-Ann Peters
Outgoing and busy. Struggles with organizing group study sessions and accessing organized materials.

#### Secondary - Kyle Johnson
Tech-savvy but frustrated by fragmented communication tools like WhatsApp groups.

#### Secondary - Zanele Ndlovu
Shy and creative. Seeks curriculum-aligned examples and confidence-building support.

### Competitors

#### Studocu
Large resource base but lacks local South African content and has unvetted quality.

#### Chegg
Expert-reviewed content but is an entirely paid service, making it expensive and irrelevant to the local curriculum.

#### Course Hero
Wide subject coverage but has inconsistent quality, few South African materials, and a confusing pricing model.

#### Quizlet
Gamified and simple but not tailored to university level depth or specific modules.

### Market Position
StudyBuddy Hub fills the gap by offering free, localized, and collaborative tools specifically for South African students.

## Visual Identity

### Logo Guidelines

#### Primary Logo (Horizontal Lockup)
The main logo representation combining icon and wordmark in horizontal arrangement.

#### Logo Variations

##### Stacked Logo
For use in vertical spaces where horizontal space is limited.

##### Icon Only
For use as a favicon or app icon at extremely small sizes where the full wordmark would be illegible.

##### Wordmark Only
For use where the icon is already established nearby or in text-heavy environments.

#### Safe Zone (Exclusion Zone)
A minimum clear space, equal to the height of the letter 'S' in the wordmark, must be maintained around all sides of the logo. No other graphic elements, text, or imagery should encroach upon this area.

#### Minimum Size
- **Digital:** 100px width for the primary horizontal logo
- **Print:** 25mm width for the primary horizontal logo
- **Icon Only:** 24px × 24px

### Colour Usage

#### Full Colour Application
- **Icon:** Owl: #2C3E50 (Navy), Cap Tassel & Books: #FFD700 (Yellow), Book Pages: #4CAF50 (Green)
- **Wordmark:** #2C3E50 (Navy)
- **Background:** White or light neutral colours

#### Reversed (Light)
Entirely white on dark backgrounds (e.g., #2C3E50 Navy).

#### Monochromatic
Solid black for single colour print or solid white for dark backgrounds.

#### Unacceptable Use
- Do not change colours, distort, rotate, or add effects like shadows
- Do not recreate or alter the icon
- Do not place on clashing or busy backgrounds

### Typography

#### Primary Font: Poppins (Bold)
Modern and clean, ideal for headings and logos.

**Usage:** Headlines, Logotype, Key CTAs

#### Secondary Font: Open Sans (Regular)
Highly readable for body text and captions.

**Usage:** Body text, captions, buttons. Use Bold for emphasis.

### Colour Palette

#### Primary Colours
- **Navy Blue:** #2C3E50 – Trust, stability, professionalism
- **Sunshine Yellow:** #FFD700 – Energy, optimism, creativity

#### Accent Colour
- **Grass Green:** #4CAF50 – Growth, community, success

### Imagery & Iconography

#### Imagery Style
- **Visual Mood:** Warm, energetic, and approachable
- **Aesthetic:** Realistic with a minimalist touch. Features diverse groups of South African students collaborating in casual, relatable settings (e.g., libraries, cafés, campus lawns)
- **Guidance:** Avoid overly staged stock photos. Use authentic, inclusive representations.

#### Iconography Style
- Icons should be simple, modern, and line-based
- Primary colour: Navy Blue (#2C3E50)
- Accent colour: Sunshine Yellow (#FFD700) for key actions
- Consistent stroke weights and corner radii
